A NOTE FROM THE CARTOGRAPHER
Take the long way.
Atlas: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a named place to learn its story.
Explore: use WASD, arrow keys, or touch buttons. Click any reachable tile to walk or sail there. Your skiff unfolds automatically on water. E or Enter interacts with nearby landmarks.
Your task: light the beacons at Mosswatch, Frostspire, and the Sunken Bell. Return to the Observatory to finish the journey.
Workshop: paint terrain, undo strokes, and export your map as plain text. Hold Space to pan, or use two fingers on touch. Imported maps use the same 280 × 116 grid; landmarks stay in place. Your journey and edits save on this device.
Music is optional. C centers the traveler. Escape stops a route or closes a conversation.
