# The First Dream

Opening fable for **Somnia**. This is a tale taught by the order, not a promise
that its account of creation is complete or correct. The goddess and her
brother remain unnamed for now.

1. Once, a goddess made a world. She set the rivers running, filled the fields
   with living things, and loved what she had made.
2. Her brother envied that love. He shattered her world and struck her down
   among its ruins.
3. As she lay dying, she dreamed it whole again. The rivers ran. The dead woke.
   And within her dream, the world endured.
4. Her brother seeks a way inside, to finish what he began. Every night, when
   we dream, we open little doors.
5. The keepers of Somnia walk those passages. They mend what he tears, and
   close what he opens. Their work has no ending.
6. Or so the tale is told. In Brackenport, a child has been asleep for six days.

Four illustrations were made with the built-in image-generation tool. Exact
prompts are in `art/fable/prompts.json`; source images and printable text
conversions are alongside them. The game draws only ASCII characters. The
illustrations dissolve into place, while the narration uses the existing
typewriter and chatter system. Each card waits for the reader to continue.

The music is an original eight-bar lullaby, "The First Dream," in 6/8. The
title and waking world carry the melody; the infected dreams alter its
harmony and selected pitches.
